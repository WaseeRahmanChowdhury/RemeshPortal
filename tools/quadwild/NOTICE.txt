RemeshPortal runs these two programs as separate processes:
  quadwild.exe and quad_from_patches.exe
The other tools of the release (cli_trace.exe, viz_mesh_results.exe) are not needed and not included.
The Microsoft C++ runtime DLLs they need (msvcp140.dll, vcruntime140.dll) are copied here by build_windows.bat.
